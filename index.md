## Four things, done properly

### Five layers of blocking

A system-wide DNS shield that covers every app and browser, a Safari content blocker built on a bundled list of hundreds of thousands of gambling domains, plus guided setup for the three defences that actually hold: operator self-exclusion, a bank gambling block, and killing every promo email and push.

[How this compares to other blockers](https://trysilex.com/best-gambling-blocker-apps/) · [The full iPhone blocking guide](https://trysilex.com/block-gambling-sites-iphone/)

### A real 90-day program

90 authored days in three phases — **See the Machine**, **Rewire**, **Fortify** — built from clinical CBT workbooks and the research on how gambling products are designed to hook you. One lesson a day, not a library to binge.

[Every source we built it from](https://trysilex.com/methodology/)

### An SOS for the worst minute

One button, always on screen, never behind the paywall. Rate the urge, breathe it down, then ride it out on a timer while the app shows you your own reasons for quitting and the money you've kept so far.

[Why urges pass, and how long it takes](https://trysilex.com/how-long-do-gambling-urges-last/)

### The money, made visible

Not gambled away. Silex takes what you told it you were putting in each week and counts up what you kept instead. It's the number that keeps people going when motivation runs out.

[Work out what gambling has cost you so far](https://trysilex.com/gambling-loss-calculator/)

## It never punishes you for slipping

Gambling recovery is not a clean line, and an app that zeroes your progress the moment you lapse teaches you to hide the lapse. Silex does the opposite. A slip triggers a **Reset day** — a specific lesson about what happened and what to change — and your history is never wiped. Milestones you've earned are never taken back.

[What to do in the hour after you gamble again](https://trysilex.com/gambling-relapse/)

## Common questions

### Is Silex free?

The safety tools are free forever — the urge button, the crisis helplines and the lapse support work without paying. The 90-day program and the protection stack need a subscription: $4.99 a week, or $49.99 a year with a 3-day free trial.

### Does Silex work on Android?

Not yet. Silex is iPhone-only, iOS 17 or later. It uses iOS-specific system APIs — DNS profiles, Safari content blockers, Screen Time — that have no direct Android equivalent.

### Can I remove the blocking in a moment of craving?

You can, and Silex is honest about that: no app can make itself truly unremovable on iOS. That is exactly why it layers five defences instead of relying on one, and why the strongest layer it points you at is operator self-exclusion, which you cannot undo on impulse.

### Does Silex send my data anywhere?

Almost nothing. There are no accounts, and everything you write stays on your phone with an optional private iCloud backup that only you can read. The app does send an anonymous count of which setup steps get finished, so the places people get stuck can be fixed — one switch in Settings turns that off.

### Is Silex medical treatment?

No. Silex helps you build healthier habits around gambling. It is not medical care and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis, use the helplines below.
