It never punishes you for slipping

Gambling recovery is not a clean line, and an app that zeroes your progress the moment you lapse teaches you to hide the lapse. Silex does the opposite. A slip triggers a Reset day: a specific lesson about what happened and what to change. Your history is never wiped, and milestones you've earned are never taken back.

What it costs, plainly

Silex is a paid app and it tells you so before you invest any time: after the intake questions you reach a paywall. $2.99 per week, or $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial (US prices; the store shows yours before you pay). Cancel any time in your Apple or Google Play account.

The crisis helplines are one tap away on the subscribe screen, free, whether or not you ever pay. If a subscription ends, the urge button, the helplines and lapse support keep working. Those are safety features, and safety features should never sit behind a payment screen.

Private because there is nothing to identify you by

No account, no email, no password, no ad network, nothing that follows you between apps. Your answers, your spending figures, your journal and your lapses live on your phone, with an optional iCloud backup encrypted to your Apple account. We cannot read any of it. The one thing Silex sends is an anonymous count of which setup steps get finished, so what trips people up can be fixed. One switch in Settings turns it off.
