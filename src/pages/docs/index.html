Silex is an iPhone app for people who want to stop gambling. It blocks gambling sites and apps across the phone, runs a 90-day quit program one day at a time, gives you an SOS button for the worst minute of an urge, and counts the money you keep. These pages explain how each part works and how to set it up.

Requirements

  • Device: iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later. Silex is iPhone only, in portrait, with a dark interface.
  • Languages: 22: English, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese.
  • Account: none. There is no sign-up and no login. Your data lives on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database.
  • Price: a subscription, $4.99 a week or $49.99 a year with a free trial. See Subscriptions.

What Silex is not

Silex is a self-help tool, not medical care. It doesn't diagnose or treat gambling disorder, and it isn't a crisis service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number or one of the free helplines listed at the bottom of every page.
