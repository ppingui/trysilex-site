Open Settings with the gear on the Today tab.

Money

  • Show money kept: show or hide money amounts across the app.
  • Goal and Goal amount: something to save towards. The Progress tab shows how far along you are, and reaching it earns the Kept Gold orb.
  • Currency: the currency amounts are shown in.

Defense

  • Re-enable notifications: asks for permission if you haven't answered yet, and sets all of Silex's reminders up again. If you turned notifications off in iOS Settings, switch them back on there first.

Support

  • Crisis resources & helplines: free lines for your region.
  • Send us a message: email the developer.
  • Restore purchases and Manage subscription.
  • Privacy policy.

Your data

  • A short statement of what is stored and where (see Privacy and your data).
  • Share anonymous usage: on by default; turn it off to stop anonymous analytics.

Not in Settings

  • Discreet notifications are set from your onboarding answers, not a switch.
  • Protection is managed on the Protect tab and in iOS Settings.
  • Notification types and sounds are in iOS Settings › Notifications › Silex.
