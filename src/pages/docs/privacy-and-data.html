Silex has no accounts and no server of its own that stores anything about you. This page is the practical summary; the privacy policy is the full, binding version.

Where your data lives

  • On your phone. Your answers, days, urges, lapses, pledges, journal and program progress are stored in the app.
  • In your private iCloud, if iCloud is on for your Apple Account. Silex mirrors its data to your own private iCloud database so it survives a new phone. Only your Apple Account can read it; we can't. Without iCloud, data stays on the phone only.

What leaves your phone

  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat). Purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID, so your subscription works and can be restored. It also receives Apple's anonymous ad-attribution token, if one exists.
  • Anonymous usage (TelemetryDeck). Which screens and steps are used, with no names, answers or amounts. Turn it off in Settings with Share anonymous usage.
  • DNS lookups, while the shield is on, go to Mullvad's DNS resolver under its no-logging policy. Silex never sees them.
  • Blocklist updates. Silex downloads the public gambling blocklist from a content-delivery network. Nothing about you is sent with the request.

Silex sends nothing you write, and nothing about the sites you visit.

Deleting your data

  1. Delete the app. That removes everything on the phone.
  2. If you used iCloud, also remove the iCloud copy: Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Silex (on some iOS versions, Manage Storage).
  3. To remove the DNS shield too: Settings › General › VPN, DNS & Device Management › DNS.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription; see Subscriptions. Silex has no data export.
