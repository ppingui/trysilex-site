All Silex notifications are scheduled on your phone; none come from a server. Turn them off any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Silex.

What Silex sends

NotificationWhen
Today's program day9:00 each morning
Evening pledge20:30 every evening, the classic urge window: a nudge to make today's pledge, or a reminder that SOS is one tap away if you already have
Risky-hour heads-up30 minutes before the hour your urge log points to, or until it can tell, the time you said urges hit hardest. Evenings are left to the pledge reminder
MilestonesThe moment you reach 3, 7, 14, 21, 30 and 90 days free, or 10:00 if that falls outside 9:00 to 21:00
Still in your cornerAfter three days without opening the app
Trial reminderTwo days before a free trial ends
After graduationA weekly Sunday check, and a check-in 30 days after you finish (day 120)

After a lapse, reminders are rescheduled.

Discreet mode

If you told Silex during onboarding that you hide your gambling, notifications use neutral wording that doesn't mention gambling, for phones others might see. If you said you hide most of it, money amounts are hidden in the app too.

Widgets

  • Days free: small, medium and large Home Screen widgets, plus circular, rectangular and inline Lock Screen widgets.
  • SOS control: a Control Center button that opens the urge flow in one press (iOS 18 or later).
  • Urge timer: a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while you ride out an urge.
