# The SOS button

An urge to gamble usually peaks and passes within about 20 to 30 minutes. The SOS flow is built to get you through that window. It is never behind the paywall, and a **Talk to someone** link to free crisis lines is on every step.

## The four steps

1. **Rate it.** *How strong is the urge right now?* on a 1 to 10 dial, then *Help me through it*.
2. **Breathe.** Three rounds of slow breathing: in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. *Skip to the wave* jumps ahead.
3. **Ride the wave.** A 10-minute urge-surfing timer. It also runs as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you can put the phone down. Tap *The urge has passed* whenever it has, or *I made it* when the timer ends.
4. **Anchor.** Your own reasons for quitting, the money you've kept (unless you hide money amounts), and up to three of your own answers from the program. Then *How's the urge now?* and *I rode it out*.

Each urge you ride out is logged in your journal. Your first earns the *First Stand* orb, and your tenth earns *Ten Stands*.

## Logging an urge without the full flow

On the Today tab, **Riding an urge?** offers *Log it*, a quick sheet to record the urge and save it, or *Ride the wave*, which opens the full flow.

## Opening SOS fast

- **In the app:** the **SOS** button floats over every tab.
- **Control Center:** add the Silex **SOS** control to open the flow in one press. Control Center controls need iOS 18 or later.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** *Open SOS*. Silex also offers *Log an urge*, *Make today's pledge* and *Days free* as shortcuts.

## If you gambled

That's a different flow. Tap **I gambled — reset with me** on the Today tab; see [what happens after a lapse](https://trysilex.com/docs/program/).

## If you need someone right now

- US: [1-800-MY-RESET](tel:18006973738) — National Problem Gambling Helpline — free, confidential, 24/7
- US: [988](tel:988) — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text if it feels like too much
- UK: [0808 8020 133](tel:08088020133) — National Gambling Helpline (GamCare) — free, confidential, 24/7
- UK: [116 123](tel:116123) — Samaritans
- Elsewhere: [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) — Crisis lines for your country
