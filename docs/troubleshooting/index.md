# Troubleshooting

Most problems are an iOS setting one step short. If none of these fixes it, email [support@trysilex.com](mailto:support@trysilex.com) with your iPhone model and iOS version. One person reads it; replies usually take a day.

## The DNS shield says "Installed", not "Active"

iOS added the configuration but it isn't selected. Open **Settings › General › VPN, DNS & Device Management › DNS** and choose **Silex Shield**. If that screen is missing or locked, a work or school management profile is in control of the phone's DNS, and the shield can't override it.

## Gambling sites still load

- Check the Protect tab: the shield should read *Active*.
- Another VPN or DNS app can take over DNS. Only one DNS setting is used at a time.
- A brand-new gambling domain may not be on any list yet. The lists update every few days.

## I can't find Silex Site Blocker in Safari

Open **Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions** (on iOS 17: **Settings › Safari › Extensions**) and turn on **Silex Site Blocker**. Open Silex once afterwards so it loads the list.

## I paid, but Silex still shows the paywall

Tap **Restore** and make sure you're signed into the Apple Account you bought with. If it still fails, email us with the purchase date.

## Notifications don't arrive

Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Silex is on, then tap **Re-enable notifications** in Silex Settings to set the reminders up again. Focus modes can hold notifications back.

## New phone

With iCloud on, your progress comes back on its own. Restore your purchase, then run the DNS shield and Safari blocker setup again: neither carries over to a new device.

## I need to talk to someone now

Don't wait for an email. The free helplines at the bottom of this page answer 24/7, and Silex's **Talk to someone** button lists the ones for your region.
