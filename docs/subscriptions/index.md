# Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Silex never sees your payment details.

## Plans

| Plan | Price (US) | Trial |
|---|---|---|
| Annual | $49.99 a year | 3-day free trial |
| Weekly | $4.99 a week | No trial, cancel any time |

Prices in other countries follow Apple's regional pricing; the paywall always shows your local price and the exact trial length before you confirm. If you close the annual payment sheet, Silex may offer the annual plan at a discount. If you bought the earlier lifetime plan, it keeps working.

## The free trial

- Nothing is charged when the trial starts.
- Silex sends a notification the day before it ends (you allow notifications just before the paywall).
- Cancel any time before it ends and you pay nothing.

## Cancelling

On your iPhone: **Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Silex › Cancel Subscription**. Silex's own Settings has a *Manage subscription* shortcut to the same place. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Restoring a purchase

New phone, or the paywall is showing even though you paid? Tap **Restore** on the paywall or **Restore purchases** in Settings, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

## Refunds

Apple handles refunds, not us: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## If your subscription ends

You need a subscription to start Silex. If it later ends, these keep working, free:

- The **SOS** urge flow and the crisis lines.
- The **lapse** flow (*I gambled — reset with me*).
- The **Today** and **Progress** tabs, and Settings.
- Blocking you've already switched on. The DNS shield and Safari blocker stay on until you turn them off in iOS Settings.

The **Program** and **Protect** tabs need an active subscription.
