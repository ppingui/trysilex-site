The 90-day program

The program is called Storm to Clarity: 90 authored days, one a day, each about four minutes. It is built from cognitive behavioural therapy for gambling and the research on how gambling products are designed; the sources are on the methodology page.

Three phases

DaysPhaseIn the app's words
1–30See the MachineIt was engineered. Drop the shame.
31–60RewireYour brain is resetting. Trust the timeline.
61–90FortifyBuild the life that competes.

Your answer to When you gamble, it's mostly to… during onboarding tunes the program to what gambling does for you, so the examples and exercises fit.

A day's lesson

Each day is a short deck of cards: reading, quizzes, sliders, fill-ins, simulators, guided exercises and a small action to take. Days 30 and 90 end with a ceremony, and days 7, 30, 60 and 90 are milestones.

How days unlock

  • One new day unlocks per calendar day. When you finish, Silex shows Done for today. Day N unlocks tomorrow. You can Read again any finished day.
  • Days never expire. If you miss a day, the program waits for you; it only moves on when you complete the current day.
  • After three or more quiet days, you're welcomed back where you left off.

After a lapse

A lapse is a moment, not the end of your recovery. Tap I gambled — reset with me on the Today tab and Silex walks you through it:

  1. What set it off? Pick the trigger.
  2. Roughly how much? The amount comes off your money-kept total.
  3. Noted. Now it's data, not a secret. Then Back on track.

What changes afterwards:

  • Your current run starts again from the lapse. Your total days free is never wiped: it keeps every gamble-free day you've had.
  • A Reset day is added before your next scheduled day. It doesn't use up a calendar day, and finishing it earns the Kintsugi orb.
  • Your reminders are rescheduled around the lapse.

The program and lessons need an active subscription. The lapse flow itself, SOS and the crisis lines don't; see what stays free.
